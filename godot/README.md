# COLLAPSIBLE TIME: THE LAST FLIGHT

A standalone Godot 4 project inside the Collapsible Time repository.

## Engine
- Godot 4.2 or newer
- GDScript
- Compatibility renderer (OpenGL-based) for older hardware

## Open the project
1. Install Godot 4 from https://godotengine.org/download/
2. Extract or clone this repository.
3. In Godot Project Manager, choose **Import**.
4. Select the `godot/project.godot` file.
5. Press F6 or F5 to run.

## Controls
- A / D or Left / Right: move
- Shift: run
- Space, W or Up: jump
- E: interact
- 1 / 2: choose at the final decision
- R: replay after the chapter ends
- Touch screens: tap the ground to move, tap the sky to jump, and tap a marked location to approach and interact. Tap after the ending to replay.
- Enter: begin

## Chapter 01: The Last Flight
Mara is ordered to deliver emergency medical supplies. On the way to her aircraft, she discovers a distress transmission that Flight Control tells her to ignore. Explore the airfield, investigate the signal, board the aircraft and decide which responsibility to honour.

The environment and character are drawn as layered paper-cutout shapes in-engine. No external assets or paid services are required.

## Project status
This is the first native-engine playable foundation. It is not yet a finished commercial game. Next production passes should add authored character animation, sound design, cinematic camera work, richer level art, accessibility options and save data.
